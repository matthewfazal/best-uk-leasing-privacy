This policy explains how we collect and use your personal information when you ask us for a car leasing quote, including through a lead form on TikTok. We are the data controller for this information, which means we decide how it is used and are responsible for looking after it.

What we collect

When you fill in one of our forms, we collect the details you give us. Depending on the form, this can include:

  • your name, email address and phone number
  • the car or type of car you are interested in
  • your rough budget, preferred contract length and mileage
  • whether you are leasing personally or for a business
  • any other information you choose to include in your message

If you fill in a form on TikTok, TikTok may also share basic technical details with us, such as the date and time you submitted the form and which advert you responded to.

How we use it

  • to respond to your request and help you find a suitable lease deal
  • to pass your enquiry to a leasing provider or broker so they can contact you with a quote
  • to contact you about your enquiry by email, phone or message
  • to keep a record of enquiries and deal with any questions or complaints

We will only send you marketing about other deals if you have agreed to it, and you can opt out at any time by emailing us.

Our legal basis

We use your information because you asked us to help you get a quote, so we process it to take steps at your request before you enter into an agreement. Where we send you marketing, we rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. We may also rely on our legitimate interests to keep records of enquiries and to run our business, where this does not override your rights.

Who we share it with

We never sell your information. We only share it where needed to handle your enquiry:

  • Leasing providers and brokers who will contact you with a quote. They will use your information under their own privacy policies.
  • TikTok, which provides the lead form you used and processes the information you enter.
  • Service providers that help us run the business, such as email and file storage providers, who may only use your information on our instructions.
  • Authorities where we are required to by law.

Transfers outside the UK

Some of the services we use, including TikTok, may store or process information outside the UK. Where this happens, we rely on the safeguards that UK data protection law allows, such as approved contract terms.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for up to 12 months after our last contact with you, then delete them, unless we need to keep them longer to deal with a complaint or meet a legal requirement.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong
  • ask us to delete your information
  • object to or ask us to limit how we use it
  • withdraw your consent to marketing at any time
  • ask us to move your information to another organisation, where this applies

To use any of these rights, email UKLeasing@outlook.com. We will reply within one month.

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including limiting who can see it and using secure, password-protected services.

Cookies

This page does not use cookies or tracking. TikTok's own pages and forms are covered by TikTok's privacy and cookie policies.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
